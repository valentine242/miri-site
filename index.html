Common questions

How does Miri choose my weights?
Starting weights come from your bodyweight and experience. After each lift, Miri uses your reps and how it felt to set the next weight. You can nudge any weight during a workout or in Settings → Workouts.
A weight feels wrong. What do I do?
Use the + and − buttons on the workout screen. Miri adjusts that lift for the rest of your plan. If a lift feels too hard three sessions in a row, Miri eases it back by about 10% on its own.
How do I cancel Miri Pro?
Open Settings → Miri Pro → Manage Subscription in the app, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Your data is kept, and your records stay free; charts come back with Pro.
How do I restore Miri Pro on a new phone?
Open Settings → Miri Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
Settings → Health & Data → Back Up, then restore that file on the new phone. You can also export a spreadsheet of every workout.
How do I delete my data?
Your data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase everything in Settings → Health & Data.
