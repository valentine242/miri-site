Miri Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Miri is a strength-training app. In short: your data stays on your device (iPhone or iPad). We don't run servers that store your workouts, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

What Miri stores, and where

  • Your profile and plan: name (optional), birth month and year, sex, height, bodyweight, goal, experience, equipment, training days and any limitations you pick (like sore knees).
  • Your health check answers: the conditions you say yes to in the setup health check (for example a heart condition, pregnancy or an eating disorder), and whether you've confirmed a doctor cleared you. Miri uses them to adjust your training and nutrition, and you can change them in Settings → Profile & Plan.
  • Your training: workouts, sets, weights, reps, how each lift felt, records, badges and Hari's items.
  • Nutrition and body tracking: calorie targets, weekly check-ins, bodyweight and measurements, if you use them.

All of this is saved on your device, inside Miri. Miri doesn't copy it to iCloud, to a server or anywhere else. A short summary (your next workout and this week's progress) is shared with Miri's own Home Screen widget on the same device. Like any app's data, it's included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you've turned those on. You can export, back up or reset everything from Settings → Health & Data at any time. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

Backups and CSV exports you make from Settings contain your data. They go wherever you save or send them (Files, AirDrop, email and so on), and they aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

If you turn on Weekly Backup (Settings → Health & Data), Miri saves a backup file once a week to a folder you choose in the Files app, such as iCloud Drive or a folder on your device. It's off unless you turn it on. Your health-check answers are included so a restore keeps your safety limits. If you pick an iCloud Drive folder, the file is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. Miri keeps the four newest weekly files there and never reads or changes your other files.

Purchases

Miri Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Miri only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns Miri Pro. Offer codes (for example from "Train with a Friend") are redeemed with Apple; Miri doesn't learn who shared or used a code.

Share cards and their captions include a link to Miri on the App Store. The link can carry a campaign tag (such as "share-record") that Apple counts in anonymous, aggregated App Store reports; it contains nothing about you.

Photos

When you save a recap or record card to Photos, Miri asks for add-only access. It can add pictures to your library but can't see, read or change anything already there.

Feedback emails

"Send Feedback" and the feedback box when you cancel open a draft in your Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The Send Feedback draft includes Miri's version, your device model and its software version, to help fix problems.

Notifications

Workout reminders, the weekly recap and the trial reminder are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Usage data

Miri does not collect usage analytics and has no third-party analytics or advertising code. Your device gives Miri Apple's performance reports (MetricKit: launch time, hangs, memory and battery use, and crash or hang diagnostics); Miri keeps the latest few on your device only and never sends them anywhere. You can share them yourself from Settings → About → Share Performance Reports, for example with support. Apple may also share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with developers if you opted in to "Share with App Developers" in your device's Settings.

Your rights

  • Access: export everything Miri holds about you from Settings → Health & Data (backup file or CSV).
  • Deletion: use Reset All Data in Settings → Health & Data, or delete the app. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it (with Reset All Data or by deleting the app). Copies you made yourself, such as backups, exports or your device's own backups, last until you delete them.

Children

Miri is meant for people 13 and older. For users under 18, Miri keeps calories at maintenance, leaves out strength comparisons, and recommends training with a qualified adult. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13, and Miri doesn't send any data to us.

Not medical advice

Miri gives general fitness guidance. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Check with a doctor before starting a new training or nutrition program, and stop any exercise that causes pain.
